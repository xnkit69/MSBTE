About MSBTE

Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education (MSBTE) is an autonomous body established by the Government of Maharashtra under the Maharashtra Act No. 29 of 1997. It is responsible for prescribing curriculum, conducting examinations and granting diplomas to students pursuing technical education across the state.

MSBTE operates under the Skill, Vocational Education, Training and Innovation Department and caters to more than nine lakh students studying in over 1,900 affiliated polytechnic institutes across all 36 districts of Maharashtra.

  • Prescribes & revises curriculum for diploma programs
  • Conducts Summer & Winter examinations
  • Issues certificates, diplomas & mark sheets
  • Affiliates and monitors polytechnic institutes
  • Promotes innovation & skill development
