General Instructions

  • Students must carry their valid Hall Ticket to the examination centre.
  • Reach the exam centre at least 30 minutes before the exam start time.
  • No electronic devices (mobile phones, calculators unless permitted) are allowed.
  • Examination timings are 11:00 AM to 2:00 PM unless stated otherwise.
  • No student will be allowed to enter after 30 minutes of exam commencement.
  • Students must follow the dress code as per institute norms.
  • Refer to the official MSBTE portal for any last-minute changes or rescheduling.
